# Unit 06 - Contact & CTA Design

Goal:
Enable users to easily contact M. Umer Majeed.

Purpose:
- Convert visitors into opportunities
- Provide clear and immediate contact options
- Reinforce professional branding

Contact Strategy:
- Keep it simple and direct
- No unnecessary forms (optional)
- Focus on fast access

Contact Options:
- Email
- LinkedIn
- GitHub

Content:
- Strong CTA message
- Short supporting line

Example tone:
"Have a project in mind or an opportunity to discuss? Let’s connect."

UI Direction:
- Clean section
- Centered layout
- Prominent CTA
- Subtle highlight (gradient or glow)

Constraints:
- No clutter
- No long forms unless needed
- Must be mobile-friendly
